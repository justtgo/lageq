About this comic

The comic humorously captures the stress of Romania’s exam season with a playful twist on the title: “National Under-Evaluation” instead of “National Evaluation.” It highlights how these exams can feel irrelevant to the younger generation.

Young Elo openly admits, “I don’t know how an analog clock works,” revealing a gap between what’s taught and what’s practical in modern life. Meanwhile, an adult complains about the lack of a legend for the clock, showing their own confusion.

The irony is clear: the adult gets stuck on unnecessary details, while Elo struggles with something fundamental. Together, they underscore the disconnect between generations and the outdated nature of some exam tasks.
