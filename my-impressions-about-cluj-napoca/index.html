My Impressions about Cluj-Napoca

george

About the Series

The idea behind this mini-series began as a way to make the most of Nora’s first visit to Cluj. We wanted to create a vlog where we could rediscover the city together, blending her fresh perspective with the unique charm of this place. However, as things unfolded, the project grew into something much more meaningful and unexpected.

This mini-series is all about showcasing a new, genuine side of Cluj. It’s not the typical touristy version of the city but a personal and authentic journey, captured through Nora’s eyes.

Context

The series follows Nora as she experiences Cluj-Napoca for the very first time. It’s a story about exploration and discovery, told across five episodes, each focusing on a different theme. "My Impressions About Cluj-Napoca"was a project created in collaboration with the amazing team at @scena_urbana.
